Lark in the Park is back on Wednesday August 5!

Our popular Lark in the Park event returns on Wednesday August 5, bringing a day packed with free activities, entertainment and family fun to celebrate National Play Day.

Taking place at Bridgford Park in West Bridgford from 10am to 4pm, the event will feature more than 40 stalls and activities for children and families to enjoy.
