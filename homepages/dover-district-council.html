£20 million Dover Pride in Place Programme »

DDC has welcomed the appointment of Nick Holbrook-Sutcliffe as Chair of the Pride in Place Board for Buckland and St Radigunds in Dover. He will lead the programme in partnership with a newly formed Board.

Telephone Payments:  The current script on our Automated Telephone Service for Sundry Debtors is incorrect. The system will prompt you to enter a 7-digit invoice number, but please enter your 12-digit reference number.

Phone and online payment service issues

We are currently experiencing issues with some telephone and online payment services. In some cases, customers may be told their payment has been declined when it has been successful. Please check with your bank to confirm whether the payment has been taken from your account before trying again. We are working to resolve the problem as quickly as possible.
