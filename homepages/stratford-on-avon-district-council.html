Important Changes to the Housing Register

Home Choice Plus will close to new Housing Register applications on 17 August 2026 as part of the transition to Stratford-on-Avon Home Choice.

Stratford-on-Avon Home Choice will open for applications on 15 September 2026. This will include a re-registration period for existing Housing Register applicants, as well as applications from new applicants.

The first properties will be advertised through the new scheme from 27 October 2026.

Local Government Reorganisation: Government decision

The Government has confirmed plans for the future of local councils in Warwickshire.

From 1 April 2028, the county’s current six Borough, District and County Councils will be replaced with two unitary councils: one covering North Warwickshire and one covering South Warwickshire.
