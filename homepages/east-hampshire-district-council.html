Make sure you're registered to vote

East Hampshire District Council is updating the electoral register this August. If you receive an email titled 'act now so East Hampshire District Council can check who is eligible to be registered to vote at your address' or letter from us, please follow the instructions and respond if required.

The annual update ensures the register remains accurate and includes everyone in your household who is eligible to vote. If you do not provide the requested information, you may be removed from the electoral register.
