    Location: The Landing, Farnborough town centre, GU14 7RT

    Cost: Free

  • Aldershot craft fayre - 15 August

    Join us for our craft fayre in Aldershot town centre.

    Start date and time: Saturday 15 August 10:00 AM

    End date and time: Saturday 15 August 3:00 PM

    Location: Union Street, Aldershot

    Cost: Free to browse the crafts

    All are welcome to our Ukraine Independence Day event, Ukraine Soul.

    Start date and time: Monday 24 August 5:00 PM

    End date and time: Monday 24 August 8:00 PM

    Location: St Peter's Church, Farnborough, GU14 7AP

    Cost: Free

News

Aldershot lido - open all summer

Our hugely popular lido is open every day throughout the summer. It's open from 10am to 6pm.

Severe Weather Emergency Protocol

Due to the current extreme heat, we have activated our Severe Weather Emergency Protocol to help protect vulnerable individuals from harm - especially those experiencing rough sleeping.

Rushmoor approves funding support for new community 3G pitch at Wavell School

Rushmoor Borough Council has approved a contribution of up to £150,000 towards a proposed new third generation artificial grass pitch at Wavell School in Farnborough.

Council reports strong progress in first quarter performance update

We are making strong progress against our 2026/27 Delivery Plan, with 18 of 23 priorities on track at the end of quarter one.
