Homepage - services panel

Our services

Council Tax

Pay your Council Tax, view your bill, manage your account, report a change and find out about discounts.

Bins and recycling

Check your bin collection day, report a missed bin collection, book a recycling centre visit, order a bulky rubbish collection and get a garden waste subscription.

Parking

Find out how to pay for parking, find where to park, buy parking permits and visitor vouchers, and pay or appeal a parking fine (PCN).

Roads and travel

Find roadworks and closures, report road problems, view and comment on open Traffic Management Orders (TMOs) and find out about accessible travel and greener travel options.

Planning and Building Control

Check if you need planning permission or Building Control approval, search or comment on a planning application and find out about heritage, conservation and regeneration plans.

Housing

Get support and advice on finding a home, manage your council tenancy, learn about rights and responsibilities as a tenant or leaseholder, get help with homelessness, and get support if you're new to the UK.

Environmental health and public issues

Report a noise or garden nuisance, report damp and mould, check a food business's hygiene rating, request a street clean, report a tree issue, get flooding advice and more.

Births, deaths and ceremonies

Find out how to register a birth or a death, give notice of a marriage or civil partnership, find ceremony venues, order certificates and find information about citizenship ceremonies.

Leisure and community

Find a local event, search for parks and play areas, find your nearest leisure centre, visit your local library or museum, find volunteering opportunities and find out how to get involved your community.

Benefits and support

Apply for a Council Tax reduction or Housing Benefit, find out about the Household Support Fund, get help with managing money, and report benefit fraud.

Care and support for adults

Services from adult social care and our partners to help you stay independent, safe and well.

Children, young people and families

Report a concern about a child or young person, find out about fostering and adoption and find the SEND Local Offer. Our children's services are provided by Achieving for Children.

Schools and learning

Apply for school places, find term dates, apply for free school meals, find out about music lessons and find courses for adults. Our children's services are provided by Achieving for Children.

Your council

Find a councillor, get information about elections and voting, find out how we make decisions, view our annual budget, make a comment or complaint, explore council jobs, and find privacy information.

Business and licensing

Find resources for running a business, including information about rates, licensing, food business registration, business premises, doing business with the Council, and economic initiatives.

Health and wellbeing

Find out how to look after your and your family's health, and get support with children’s health, information about mental health, dementia, sexual health, drug and alcohol problems, weight loss and stopping smoking.

Safety, crime and emergencies

Find guidance on reporting a crime or antisocial behaviour, find domestic and sexual violence support, get advice on staying safe online and find advice on local safety issues.

Climate and sustainability

Find out about our Climate Action Strategy, get involved in the Kingston Green Business Community forum, and find out about saving energy and money at home.

Kingston’s mentoring programme bridges the gap for local architecture students

Aspiring architects, urban designers and landscape architects at Kingston University are gaining rare, real-world insight into the planning system thanks to an innovative mentoring programme developed by Design South East for Kingston Council.

21 July 2026

Youth Futures Summit comes to Kingston as we work to empower the next generation with skills and local career opportunities

Kingston Council is fully committed to supporting young people into meaningful employment and skills development, joining forces with regional partners at the South London Partnership (SLP) Youth Futures Summit and celebrating a number of successful work experience programmes.

20 July 2026

Kingston Council calls for urgent national funding to help protect communities from climate change

Kingston Council is calling on the government to urgently step up funding for local authorities to help protect residents, secure infrastructure, and bridge the widening gap between net zero targets and council capacities to respond to the changing climate.
