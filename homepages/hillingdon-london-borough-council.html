Hillingdon People

The magazine for Hillingdon residents, which provides community news from the council, is delivered to homes across the borough 4 times a year.

31 July 2026

Council helps to deliver new home for Uxbridge Post Office

Two temporary Post Office counters are due to open in the Civic Centre in Uxbridge on Thursday 6 August from 9am, after Hillingdon Council stepped in to ensure the continuation of the vital service following the closure of the High Street branch in May.

30 July 2026

Council challenges Mayor's plan over significant threat to Hillingdon's green belt

Hillingdon Council has announced its opposition to the Mayor of London's draft London Plan, warning that it would place a disproportionate burden on the borough by requiring more green belt development than anywhere else in London while failing to address the uncertainty surrounding Heathrow expansion.
