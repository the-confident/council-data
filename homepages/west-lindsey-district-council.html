Annual Electoral Canvass 2026

We will be contacting households in West Lindsey from the 6 July 2026. If we hold an email address for you, you will receive an email titled “Annual Electoral Canvass 2026”, this will require a response. If we do not hold an email, you will receive a Canvass Communication letter. These will be labeled in the top right with either CCA, which only needs a response if changes are to be made, or CCB, which requires a response.
