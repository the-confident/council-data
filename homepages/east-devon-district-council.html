PCN scam message

Please be aware that we have received reports of "scam" text messages relating to PCN payments that are due for urgent payment. We NEVER send out text messages relating to PCN's and you will always find a physical ticket attached to your vehicle if you have received one. Please ignore and report any unsolicited text messages to Action Fraud
