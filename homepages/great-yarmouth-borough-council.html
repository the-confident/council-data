      People are being invited to attend a service of thanksgiving and remembrance in Great Yarmouth to mark the anniversary of VJ Day.

      Waste collection, street cleaning and litter bins being emptied as usual as 56 workers take strike action

      As a week-long strike of some Great Yarmouth Services staff got underway today (August 6), the council said all five of its regular waste collection rounds are taking place, litter bins are being emptied and street cleaning is taking place as usual.
