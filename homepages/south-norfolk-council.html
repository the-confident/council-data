South Norfolk garden waste collections

Collections due between Monday 3 August and Friday 7 August will be collected one day later than usual, from Tuesday 4 August to Saturday 8 August. Please leave your garden waste bin out by 6:30am on your revised collection day. Collections will return to normal from Monday 10 August.
