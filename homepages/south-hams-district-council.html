Visit our engagement portal

Our consultation portal is the place to find all of our active, completed and upcoming consultations, where you can share your views and have your say on what we're doing in your area.

The portal is shared between South Hams and West Devon Council, so please select your area from the tiles on the homepage.

You can also sign up for newsletters and updates about what's happening near you.
